## Laravel Boost Projects

- `AGENTS.md` in this repo is generated by Laravel Boost (`php artisan boost:install`)
  — don't hand-edit it or duplicate its content here; refresh it by re-running Boost
  after a package upgrade instead.
- Domain/business-logic rules that only apply to specific paths belong in
  `.ai/rules/<name>.md` with a matching glob row in `.ai/rules/index.md` — keep
  those local to the repo. Only conventions shared across repos belong here in
  shared-workflows.
- Pint/Pest are the enforcement mechanisms for style and correctness — AI review
  should not re-litigate what they already check (see PR hygiene above).
